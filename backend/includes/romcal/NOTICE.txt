Romcal.php and fixed.dat in this directory are a PHP port of, and data for,
ROMCAL, the General Roman Calendar generator originally written in C by
Kenneth G. Bath (http://www.romcal.net). The original notice follows and
applies to this port and to the data file. Use is restricted to
non-commercial applications.
